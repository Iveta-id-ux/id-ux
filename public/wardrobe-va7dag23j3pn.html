Zone Wardrobe

Every outfit and mask in MISERY, laid out flat like a catalogue page. Pick one jacket and one mask.

  • 1 Choose a jacket, 1–7
  • 2 Choose a mask, M1–M5
  • 3 Send Gokaroth the two numbers
Most of these are the real garment photographed and laid flat, so they look like a clothes listing. Two of them — the hazmat suits — are the 3D model pieces flattened out instead, which looks strange but is the same outfit on a character.

Jackets

Body, legs and headwear together. This is the bulk of how you’ll look.

Masks

All Soviet-pattern respirators. The big shape is the faceplate you’ll actually see; the smaller bits around it are the straps, filter and hose.
