How the scan worked

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A small crawler (codename Hunter) ran the whole pipeline end-to-end:

  • Read the CV once into a structured profile (skills, seniority, domains, languages). The CV is from 2024, so it was read as a lightly-enriched, present-day profile — seniority isn't penalised for the document's age.
  • Searched live boards for the target titles — Product Designer, UX Strategy, Product Strategy, UX Design Manager — using token matching so multi-word titles match in any order.
  • Two-pass scoring. A fast model scored every listing 0–100 against the CV and classified its contract type; a stronger model then wrote tailored notes only for the strong matches (so cost scales with quality, not volume).
  • Kept consultancy/contractor roles on purpose — plenty of good offers simply skip the admin of local full-time hiring.

Title coverage. On the reliable boards, Product Designer and Product Strategy returned matches; UX Strategy and UX Design Manager as exact phrasings returned almost nothing. Broadening to Product Designer, Senior/Lead Product Designer, Head of Design, UX Lead widens the funnel considerably.

Jobs.bg is worth the manual step. It's the richest Bulgaria-local source and produced the single best match. Until it's reached via a residential proxy, the reliable route is to copy the page text and let the matcher score it — exactly how the Telerik role was found.

Honest caveats. This is one snapshot of recent listings, not the whole market. Scores come from a single model pass — directional, not gospel. And HN's free-text posts parse imperfectly, so those two titles were hand-checked against the source.
